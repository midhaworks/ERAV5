02 · Demand versus evidence

A target is not
a data inventory.

Required exposure follows the live sliders and the selected phase budget: 18T for main/naive, 2T for annealing. Supply is shown only where a published token count is comparable; “unmeasured” is preferable to wishful arithmetic.

Published discovery sizes still require V5 tokenisation, licence filtering and cross-source deduplication before becoming accepted supply. Long-context documents are charged only to the long-context lane, never counted twice.

Indic inventory check · Bhashini

Bhashini is not one translation dataset.

04 · Curriculum

Earn complexity
in stages.

Context, task difficulty and reasoning depth grow separately. Progression requires held-out evidence, not merely elapsed tokens.

0–20%Foundation

8K context. D0/D1 = 80%. Local code edits, direct facts and short native-language generation.

20–60%Composition

Grow to 32K. Introduce multi-file code, short tool workflows and R2 reasoning.

60–90%Operation

Grow to 64K. Full repositories, failure recovery, multi-document reasoning and target difficulty mix.

90–100%Anneal

128K only after RULER passes. High-quality scarce lanes, harder tasks and no unverified Indic.

Promotion ruleDo not increase context or reasoning length unless the current band improves its target benchmark without regressing short-context or easy-task performance.
