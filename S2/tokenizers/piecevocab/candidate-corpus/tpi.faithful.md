**Republik bilong India** i wanpela kantri bilong [Saut Esia](https://tpi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saut_Esia?action=edit&redlink=1 "Saut Esia"). Long not-westen sait, em i gat arere wantaim [Pakistan](https://tpi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan "Pakistan"), long not-isten sait wantaim [Nepal](https://tpi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal "Nepal"), [Bhutan](https://tpi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhutan "Bhutan") na [Saina](https://tpi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saina "Saina"), na long isten sait wantaim [Banglades](https://tpi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh") na [Burma](https://tpi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burma?action=edit&redlink=1 "Burma"). Kapitol bilong India emi [New Deli](https://tpi.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Delhi?action=edit&redlink=1 "New Delhi").
