**Eynda** yw repoblek veur yn [Asi Soth](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asi_Soth "Asi Soth"). Gans moy ages mil vilvil a dus, hi yw brassa gwerinieth an [Norvys](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norvys "Norvys"). Yma 28 stat ha 7 tiredh y'n kesunyans. Yn-mysk hy hentrevogyon yma [Pakistan](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan "Pakistan"), [Nepal](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal "Nepal"), [Bangladesh](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh"), ha [China](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/China "China"). Peder sita pur vras yw [Mumbai](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai "Mumbai"), [Kolkata](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata "Kolkata"), [Delhi](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi "Delhi"), ha [Chennai](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai "Chennai"). Yn-mysk an sitys erell a vri, merkyewgh [Bengalour](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengalour "Bengalour"), [Hyderabad](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad "Hyderabad"), [Kanpur](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanpur "Kanpur"), Ludhiana h.e.

Yma mynydhyow Himalaya yn gogledh an wlas; a-is yth ystynn plenys an Indus ha'n Ganga.

Yma kryjyansow Eyndek ow synsi ynna [Hindoueth](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindoueth "Hindoueth") hag [Islam](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam "Islam"), [Kristoneth](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristoneth "Kristoneth") ha [Bouddhisteth](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bouddhisteth "Bouddhisteth"), Sikheth, Zoroastrianeth, ha lies kryjyans arall. An wlas ma yw an tyller le may tallathas an kryjyansow Jayna, Bouddhistek, Hindou ha Sikh.

Taves chyf Eynda Gledh yw [Hindik](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi "Hindi"), mes yma [yethow ranndiryel](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeth_ranndiryel "Yeth ranndiryel") erell ha gansa milyonow pals a gewsoryon: Marathek, Telougou, [Tamil](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil "Tamil"), [Bengalek](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengalek "Bengalek"), Goujaratek, Kannadek, Panjabi, Oriya, Malayalam, Assamek, Maythili, Kashmiri, h.e. Yma studh soedhogel dhe'n tavosow ma, warbarth gans [Sowsnek](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sowsnek "Sowsnek") (kewsys gans lies person avel nessa yeth), Sindhi, Nepalek, Dogari, Santali ha [Sanskrit](https://kw.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit "Sanskrit").
