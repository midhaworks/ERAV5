Drom ke Barata

**Barata** tir patectapa ke geefa [Asia](https://avk.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia "Asia") dem [New Delhi](https://avk.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Delhi?action=edit&redlink=1 "New Delhi") kelu. [[1]](https://avk.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barata#cite_note-1)

Vegungafa patecta tid [Bangladeca](https://avk.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladeca "Bangladeca") is [Sinia](https://avk.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinia "Sinia") is [Butana](https://avk.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butana?action=edit&redlink=1 "Butana") is [Srilanka](https://avk.wikipedia.org/wiki/Srilanka?action=edit&redlink=1 "Srilanka") is [Nepala](https://avk.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepala?action=edit&redlink=1 "Nepala") is [Afgana](https://avk.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afgana "Afgana") is [Pakistana](https://avk.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistana?action=edit&redlink=1 "Pakistana") is [Myanma](https://avk.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanma?action=edit&redlink=1 "Myanma").
