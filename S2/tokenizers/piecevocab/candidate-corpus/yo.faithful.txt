**Índíà**, tàbí **Orílẹ̀-èdè** **Olómìnira ilẹ̀ Índíà** (ní èdè [Híńdì](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Èdè_Híndì "Èdè Híndì"): भारत गणराज्य ***Bhārat** Gaṇarājya*;), jẹ́ orílẹ̀-èdè kan ní Gúúsù Éṣíà. Ohun ni orílẹ̀-èdè [kejè títóbi júlọ̀](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_outlying_territories_by_total_area "List of countries and outlying territories by total area") gẹ́gẹ́ bí ìtóbí jẹ́ọ́gráfì, orílẹ̀-èdè [kìn-ín-ni tó ní ìye àwọn ènìyàn](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_population "List of countries by population") [júlọ](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_outlying_territories_by_total_area "List of countries and outlying territories by total area")[[11]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-:0-11) pẹ̀lú bíi 1.43 bílíọ̀nù ènìyàn,[[12]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-:1-12) àti ìjọba-oníbò tó ni ìye àwọn ènìyàn júlọ̀ lágbàyé. Ó ja mọ [Òkun Índíà](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Ocean "Indian Ocean") ní gúúsù, [Òkun Lárúbáwá](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabian_Sea "Arabian Sea") ni ìwọ̀ oòrùn, àti [Ebado Benga](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_of_Bengal?action=edit&redlink=1 "Bay of Bengal") ní ìlà oòrùn, India ní etíkun tó jẹ́ 7,516.6 kilometres (4,700 mi)[[13]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-:2-13). Ó ni ààlà láàrin [Pakistan](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan "Pakistan") ní ìwọ̀ oòrùn; [Ṣáínà](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ṣáínà "Ṣáínà"), [Nepal](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal "Nepal"), ati [Bhutan](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhutan "Bhutan") si àríwà; ati [Bangladẹ́shì](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh") ati [Burma](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burma "Burma") ní ìlà oòrùn. Índíà wà nítosí [Sri Lanka](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka "Sri Lanka"), àti [Maldives](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maldives "Maldives") tí wọ́n wà ní [Òkun Índíà](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Òkun_Índíà "Òkun Índíà").

Gẹ́gẹ́ bíi ilẹ̀ Ọ̀làjú Àfonífojì Indus jẹ́ ibí tó ní ìdàgbàsókè púpọ̀, abẹ́orílẹ̀ Índíà jẹ́ ibi àwọn ènìyàn mọ̀ fún ọlá àti [àṣà](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Àṣà "Àṣà") rẹ̀ káàkiri ayé.[[12]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-:1-12) Esin nla merin, [Hinduism](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism?action=edit&redlink=1 "Hinduism"), [Buddhism](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism "Buddhism"), [Jainism](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jainism?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jainism") ati [Sikhism](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikhism?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sikhism") ni won bere latibe, nigbati [Zoroastrianism](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoroastrianism?action=edit&redlink=1 "Zoroastrianism"), [Esin Ju](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judaism?action=edit&redlink=1 "Judaism"), [Esin Kristi](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity "Christianity") ati [Imale](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam "Islam") de sibe ni egberundun akoko IO (CE) won si kopa ninu bi orisirisi [asa](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_culture?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian culture") agbegbe na seri. Diedie o je fifamora latowo [British East India Company](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honourable_East_India_Company?action=edit&redlink=1 "Honourable East India Company") lati ibere orundun ikejidinlogun, o si di ile amusin Ile-oba Isodokan lati arin orundun ikandinlogun, India di orile-ede alominira ni 1947 leyin [akitiyan fun isominira](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_independence_movement?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian independence movement") to se pataki fun [isatako alaise jagidijagan](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-violent_resistance?action=edit&redlink=1 "Non-violent resistance") kakiri.[[14]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-CONCISE_ENCYCLOPEDIA_3-14)

India je orile-ede olominira kan to ni [ipinle 28 ati awon agbegbe isokan meje](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_territories_of_India "States and territories of India") pelu [sistemu onileasofin oseluaralu](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_democracy?action=edit&redlink=1 "Parliamentary democracy"). [Okowo India](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Economy of India") ni [okowo ikokanla titobijulo](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_GDP_(nominal) "List of countries by GDP (nominal)") gege bi olorujo [GDP](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product "Gross domestic product") lagbaye ati [ikerin titobijulo](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_GDP_(PPP) "List of countries by GDP (PPP)") gege bi [ibamu agbara iraja](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purchasing_power_parity?action=edit&redlink=1 "Purchasing power parity"). Otun je omo egbe [Ajoni awon Orile-ede](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Nations "Commonwealth of Nations"), [G-20](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/G20_major_economies?action=edit&redlink=1 "G20 major economies"), [BRIC](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/BRIC?action=edit&redlink=1 "BRIC"), [SAFTA](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Asian_Free_Trade_Area?action=edit&redlink=1 "South Asian Free Trade Area") ati [Agbajo Owo Agbaye](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Organization?action=edit&redlink=1 "World Trade Organization"). India je [orile-ede toni ohun-ijagun bombu inuatomu](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_states_with_nuclear_weapons?action=edit&redlink=1 "List of states with nuclear weapons"), o si ni [ile-ise ologun ikewa ton nawojulo](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_military_expenditures?action=edit&redlink=1 "List of countries by military expenditures") pelu [ile-ise ologun adigun keji titobijulo](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_number_of_troops?action=edit&redlink=1 "List of countries by number of troops") lagbaye. [Atunse okowo to bere lati 1991](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_liberalization_in_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Economic liberalization in India") ti so India di ikan ninu [awon okowo to n dagba kiakia julo lagbaye](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_GDP_(real)_growth_rate?action=edit&redlink=1 "List of countries by GDP (real) growth rate");[[15]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-ERS-15) sibesibe, [aini](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_in_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Poverty in India") si tun ba ja,[[16]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-gov.in-prmar07PDF-16) [aimookomooka](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Education in India"), [iwa-ibaje](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corruption_in_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Corruption in India"), [arun](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_in_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Health in India"), ati [aijeunkanu](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malnutrition_in_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Malnutrition in India"). Gege bi awujo [esin olopolopo](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religious_pluralism?action=edit&redlink=1 "Religious pluralism"), [eledepupo](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Languages_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Languages of India") ati [eleya eniyan pupo](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiethnic_society?action=edit&redlink=1 "Multiethnic society"), India tun je ibugbe fun opolopo awon [eran-igbe](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fauna_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Fauna of India") ni opolopo [ibi alaabo](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protected_areas_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Protected areas of India").

Atijo](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Persian?action=edit&redlink=1 "Old Persian") *[Hindu](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu?action=edit&redlink=1 "Hindu")*, lati [Sanskrit](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sanskrit") सिन्धु *Sindhu*, oruko ibile fun [Odo Indus](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus_River?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indus River").[[11]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-:0-11) Awon [Griiki](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griiki?action=edit&redlink=1 "Griiki") ayeijoun n pe awon ara India ni *Indoi* (Ινδοί), ## Ìtàn

Awon ibugbe apata Igba Okuta pelu awon iyaworan ni [Bhimbetka rock shelters](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhimbetka_rock_shelters?action=edit&redlink=1 "Bhimbetka rock shelters") ni [Madhya Pradesh](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhya_Pradesh "Madhya Pradesh") ni eri igbe eniyan pipejulo ni India. Awon ibudo akoko ti a mo bere ni odun to ju 9,000 seyin lo, diedie o si dagba soke si [Indus Valley Civilisation](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus_Valley_Civilisation?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indus Valley Civilisation"),[[19]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-19) ti ojo ori re to odun 3400 [BCE](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Era "Common Era") ni apaiwoorun India. Eyi je titele pelu [Vedic period](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vedic_period?action=edit&redlink=1 "Vedic period"), to se ipilese [Esin Hindu](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism?action=edit&redlink=1 "Hinduism") ati awon asa miran igba ibere awujo India, o si pari ni awon odun 500 BCE. Lati bi 550 BCE, opolopo awon ile-oba alominira ati orile-ede olominira ti a mo bi [Mahajanapadas](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahajanapadas?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mahajanapadas") je didasile kakiri ibe.[[20]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-20)

[![Damaged brown painting of a reclining man and woman.](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/30/Indischer_Maler_des_6._Jahrhunderts_001.jpg/250px-Indischer_Maler_des_6._Jahrhunderts_001.jpg)](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fáìlì:Indischer_Maler_des_6._Jahrhunderts_001.jpg)

Paintings at the [Ajanta Caves](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajanta_Caves?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ajanta Caves") in [Aurangabad](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurangabad,_Maharashtra?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aurangabad, Maharashtra"), [Maharashtra](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharashtra "Maharashtra"), sixth century

Ni orundun keta KIO (BCE), opo gbogbo Guusu Asia je piparapo di [Ileobaluaye Maurya](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurya_Empire?action=edit&redlink=1 "Maurya Empire") latowo [Chandragupta Maurya](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandragupta_Maurya?action=edit&redlink=1 "Chandragupta Maurya") o si lokunkun ni asiko [Ashoka Olokiki](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashoka_the_Great?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ashoka the Great").[[21]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-21) Lati igba orundun keta IO (CE), [ijoba iran Gupta](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gupta_Empire?action=edit&redlink=1 "Gupta Empire") samojuto igba ti a mo bi ancient "[Igba Oniwura India](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Age_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Golden Age of India") ayeijoun."[[22]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-22)[[23]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-23) Awon ileobaluaye ni [Apaguusu India](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "South India") je kikopomo gbogbo awon ti [Chalukya](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalukya_dynasty?action=edit&redlink=1 "Chalukya dynasty"), ti [Cholas](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chola_Dynasty?action=edit&redlink=1 "Chola Dynasty") ati ti [Vijayanagara Empire](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijayanagara_Empire?action=edit&redlink=1 "Vijayanagara Empire"). [Sayensi, teknoloji](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_and_technology_in_ancient_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Science and technology in ancient India"), [ise-ero](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Indian_inventions_and_discoveries?action=edit&redlink=1 "List of Indian inventions and discoveries"), [ise-ona](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_art?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian art"), [ogbon](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_logic?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian logic"), [ede](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Languages_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Languages of India"), [litireso](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_literature?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian literature"), [mathematiiki](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_mathematics?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian mathematics"), [itorawo](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_astronomy?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian astronomy"), [esin](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_religion?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian religion") ati [imo-oye](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_philosophy?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian philosophy") di olokunkun labe itoju awon oba wonyi.

Leyin awon [iborile lati Arin Asia](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim_conquest_in_the_Indian_subcontinent?action=edit&redlink=1 "Muslim conquest in the Indian subcontinent") larin orundun 10th ati 12th, opo Ariwa India ni won bo si abe ijoba [Delhi Sultanate](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Sultanate?action=edit&redlink=1 "Delhi Sultanate") ati leyin eyi sabe [Ileobaluaye Mughal](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mughal_Empire?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mughal Empire"). Labe isejoba [Akbar the Great](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akbar_the_Great?action=edit&redlink=1 "Akbar the Great"), India gbadun idagbasoke asa ati okowo pupo ati irepo esin.[[24]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-24)[[25]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-25) Awon obaluaye Mughal diedie fe ile won si pupo lati dori apa nla abeorile. Sibesibe, ni [Ariwa-Apailaorun](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/North-East_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "North-East India") India, alagbara to joba ibe ni ileoba [Ahom](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahom?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ahom") ti [Assam](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assam "Assam"), gege bi ikan ninu awon ileoba ti won lodi si ijobalenilori Mughal. Ihalemo ninla akoko si agbara ijoba Mughal wa latodo oba [Hindu](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu?action=edit&redlink=1 "Hindu") [Rajput](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajput "Rajput") kan [Maha Rana Pratap](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maha_Rana_Pratap?action=edit&redlink=1 "Maha Rana Pratap") ti [Mewar](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mewar?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mewar") ni orundun 16th ati leyin eyi latodo orile-ede Hindu kan to n je [adapapo Maratha](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maratha_confederacy?action=edit&redlink=1 "Maratha confederacy"), to joba lori opo India ni arin orundun 18th.[[26]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-26)

Lati orundun 16th, awon alagbara ara Europe bi [Portugal](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal "Portugal"), [Hollandi](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands "Netherlands"), [Fransi](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/France "France"), ati [Iparapo Ileoba](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom "United Kingdom") se idasile ibudo owo (trading posts) be sini leyin eyi won lo anfani ti awon ija abele ibe fa wa lati sedasile awon ibiamusin nibe. Nigba to fi di 1856, opo India ti bo sabe idari [British East India Company](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honourable_East_India_Company?action=edit&redlink=1 "Honourable East India Company").[[27]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-27) Odun kan leyin eyi, igbogundi kakiri awon eyo ologun olodi ijoba ati awon ileoba, ti a mo bi [Ogun Ilominira Akoko India](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Rebellion_of_1857?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian Rebellion of 1857") tabi [Sepoy Mutiny](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Rebellion_of_1857?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian Rebellion of 1857"), koju idari ile-ise yi gidigidi sugbon ko yori si rere. Nitori aidurorege, India bo si abe isejoba taara [Oba Alade Britani](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_the_United_Kingdom?action=edit&redlink=1 "Monarchy of the United Kingdom").

A half dozen other people are in the background.](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f2/Nehru_Gandhi_1937.jpg/250px-Nehru_Gandhi_1937.jpg)](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fáìlì:Nehru_Gandhi_1937.jpg)

Ni orundun 20th, [akitiyan fun ilominira](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_independence_movement?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian independence movement") kakiri orile-ede bere latowo [Indian National Congress](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Congress?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian National Congress") ati awon agbajo oloselu miran.[[28]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-28) Olori India [Mahatma Gandhi](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohandas_Karamchand_Gandhi "Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi") lewaju awon egbegberun eniyan ni opolopo igbese torile-ede fun [aigboran araalu](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_disobedience?action=edit&redlink=1 "Civil disobedience") [aini-jagidijagan](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahimsa?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ahimsa") .[[14]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-CONCISE_ENCYCLOPEDIA_3-14)

Ni ojo 15 osu kejo 1947, India gba ilominira kuro lowo isejoba Britani, sugbon nigba kanna awon [agbegbe ogunlogo Musulum je pipinniya](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Partition of India") lati da orile-ede otooto [Pakistan](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan "Pakistan").[[29]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-CONCISE_ENCYCLOPEDIA..-29) Ni ojo 26 osi kinni 1950, India di orile-ede olominira be sini [ilana ibagbepo](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Constitution of India") tuntun je gbigbe jade.[[30]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-CIA-30)

Lati igba ilominira, India ti ni awon isoro lati owo[jagidijagan esin](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religious_violence_in_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Religious violence in India"), [casteism](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caste-related_violence_in_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Caste-related violence in India"), [naxalism](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naxalite?action=edit&redlink=1 "Naxalite"), [isedaniloro](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorism_in_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Terrorism in India") ati latowo awon igbogunti oludase agbegbe, agaga ni [Jammu ati Kashmir](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurgency_in_Jammu_and_Kashmir?action=edit&redlink=1 "Insurgency in Jammu and Kashmir") ati Ariwailaorun India. Lati igba awon odun 1990 awon [idigbolu adaniloro](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Àdàkọ:Campaignbox_India_terrorism?action=edit&redlink=1 "Àdàkọ:Campaignbox India terrorism") ti nipa lori opolopo awon ilu India. India ni ariyanjiyan ile pelu orile-ede [Olominira awon Eniyan ile Saina](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Republic_of_China "People's Republic of China"), to di ni odun 1962 [Ogun Saina-India](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sino-Indian_War?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sino-Indian War"), ati pelu [Pakistan](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan "Pakistan"), to fa awon ogun ni [1947](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Pakistani_War_of_1947?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indo-Pakistani War of 1947"), [1965](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Pakistani_War_of_1965?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indo-Pakistani War of 1965"), [1971](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Pakistani_War_of_1971?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indo-Pakistani War of 1971") ati ni [1999](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kargil_War?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kargil War"). India je omo egbe adasile [Iparapo awon Orile-ede](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations "United Nations") (gege bi India Britani) ati [Non-Aligned Movement](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Aligned_Movement?action=edit&redlink=1 "Non-Aligned Movement"). Ni odun 1974, India se [nuclear test](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smiling_Buddha?action=edit&redlink=1 "Smiling Buddha") nipamo[[31]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-India_is_a_Nuclear_State-31) ati [idanwo marun](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pokhran-II?action=edit&redlink=1 "Pokhran-II") ni 1998, eyi so India di [orile-ede to ni bombu inuatomu](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_states_with_nuclear_weapons?action=edit&redlink=1#Other_known_nuclear_powers "List of states with nuclear weapons").[[31]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-India_is_a_Nuclear_State-31) Lati 1991, [atundase okowo pataki](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_liberalization_in_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Economic liberalization in India")[[32]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-Montek-32) ti so India di [ikan ninu awon okowo to n dagba kiakia julo](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_GDP_(real)_growth_rate?action=edit&redlink=1 "List of countries by GDP (real) growth rate") lagbaye, eyi fun lagbara pupo laye.[[15]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-ERS-15)

## Ijoba

Àyọkà olórí: [Government of India](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Government of India")

[Ilana-Ibagbepo ile India](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Constitution of India"), to je ilana-ibagbepo togunjulo ati to kunrerejulo ti orile-ede alominira lagbaye, je gbigba bi ofin ni 26 January 1950.[[33]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-Pylee2004-33) [Akokoso](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preamble_to_the_Constitution_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Preamble to the Constitution of India") [ilana-ibagbepo](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Constitution of India") yi setumo India gege bi orile-ede [olominira](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic "Republic") [toseluaralu](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_democracy?action=edit&redlink=1 "Liberal democracy") [sovereign](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sovereignty?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sovereignty"), [sosialisti](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialism "Socialism"), [ti araaye](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secularism?action=edit&redlink=1 "Secularism").[[34]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-Dutt1998-34) India ni ileasofin oniyewumeji to n sise bi sistemu onileasofin [iru Westminster](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster_system?action=edit&redlink=1 "Westminster system"). Iru ijoba re je jijuwe pe o je bi 'quasi-federal' pelu gbongan to lagbara ati awon ipinle ti won ko lagbara,[[35]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-Wheare1964-35) sugbon o ti di apapo diedie lati opin awon odun 1990 nitori awon iyipada oloselu, olokowo ati alawujo.[[36]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-dencentralisation-36)

[Aare ile India](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "President of India") ni olori orile-ede[[37]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-Sharma1950-37) to je didiboyan latowo [igbimo onidiboyan](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_college?action=edit&redlink=1 "Electoral college")[[38]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-38) fun igba odun marun kan.[[39]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-39)[[40]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-40) [Alakoso Agba](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Prime Minister of India") ni olori ijoba, ohun lo si segbese opo agbara alase.[[37]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-Sharma1950-37) Gege bi yiyan latowo Aare,[[41]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-41)Alakoso Agba je titileyin latowo [egbe oloselu](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party "Political party") tabi ifowosowopo oloselu to ni ogunlogo awon ijoko ni ile kekere Ileasofin.[[37]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-Sharma1950-37) Apa ijoba apase ni Aare, Igbakeji Aare, ati [Igbimo awon Alakoso](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_Republic_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Council of Ministers of Republic of India") ([Kabinet](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_(government)?action=edit&redlink=1 "Cabinet (government)") ni igbimo apase re) ti olori re je Alakoso Agba. Alakoso yiowu to ni ipo gbodo je ikan ninu awon omo ile ileasofin. Ninu sistemu onileasofin ti India, apase wa labe asofin, nibi ti Alakoso Agba ati Igbimo re wa labe ile kekere.[[42]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-manorama-42)

Asofin ile India ni [Ileasofin](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Parliament of India") oniyewumeji, to ni ile oke ti o n je [Rajya Sabha](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajya_Sabha?action=edit&redlink=1 "Rajya Sabha") (Igbimo awon Ipinle) ati ile kekere to n je [Lok Sabha](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lok_Sabha?action=edit&redlink=1 "Lok Sabha") (Ile awon Eniyan).[[43]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-43) Rajya Sabha, agbarajo aiyese, ni 245 omo egbe ti won wa nibe fun odun marun.[[44]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-Parliament-44) Opo won je didiboyan taara latowo awon asofin [ipinle ati agbegbe](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_territories_of_India "States and territories of India") gege bi iye awon eniyan won.[[44]](https://yo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Índíà#cite_note-Parliament-44)
