**India** (eyi ekigwen si **Industan** mè **Barat** me emen ido ya) ìre ido me [Esia agan̄ Osiki](https://ann.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esia_agan̄_Osiki?action=edit&redlink=1 "Esia agan̄ Osiki"). Ọmọ ore ido òmimin ichit me linyọn̄ ire efuk otu-ifuk ebi ìluluk me emen, mè ire òso jaaba òmimin ichit me okike ijọn̄. Ido ìtatap ikana ọmọ ìre Pakistan me agan̄ inyon̄ ichep-ura; [Chaina](https://ann.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaina?action=edit&redlink=1 "Chaina"), Nepalu mè [Butan](https://ann.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butan?action=edit&redlink=1 "Butan") me agan̄ inyọn̄; mè Bangaladish mè Mayanima me agan̄ mbum-ura. Siri Lanka ìkup igbet ọmọ me agan̄ osiki
