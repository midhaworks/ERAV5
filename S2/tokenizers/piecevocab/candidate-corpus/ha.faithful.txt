## Tarihi:

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c8/Taj_Mahal_in_March_2004.jpg/250px-Taj_Mahal_in_March_2004.jpg)](Fayil:Taj_Mahal_in_March_2004.jpg)

[Taj Mahal](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taj_Mahal "Taj Mahal") a garin Agra (Uttar Pradesh)

Masanin falsafa dan kasar [Indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya) [Gautama Buddha](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gautama_Buddha "Gautama Buddha") ya kafa addinin Buddah a Indiya.

Tarihi ya nuna [indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya) tana da wasu kayayyaki tun na da can, dakin dabewa ya kara da nuna cewa an samo alamun mutunen farko a [Indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya) tun a bayan shekara ta (9,000) tun daga karni na biyar kafin haihuwar [Annabi Isah](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isah "Isah") sun yi masarautu da yawa kamar masarautar hawariya a arewacin

Kasar ita ce mafi muhimmanci sarakunanta na farko shi ne [Ashoka](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashoka "Ashoka") yana bin addinin bosa, a farkon shekara ta
Dari da tamanin (180) kafin haihuwar [Annabi Isah](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annabi_Isah "Annabi Isah") ikrikawa da bartiyawa suka mamaye [Indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya) a karni na goma sha uku sai masarautar kwace mulkin. Daga kudanci ma masarautu da yawa suna da rikici , masarautar tashiras tafi karfi kuma a zamaninsu ne kasar [Indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya) ta samu ingancin Ilimin zane-zane da sauransu.

A zamanin mafiya yawa Musulmai ne suka fara tunani a kan [Indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya), a wannan lokaci suka masu daula a gefen kogin sinda suka ajiye dakarun su a [Afghanistan](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghanistan "Afghanistan") sannan suka faɗa [Delhi](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi "Delhi") suka masu masarauta a can ita ce masarautar Musulunci ta farko a [indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya), a shekara ta dubu daya da dari uku da hamsin (1350) masarautar taglag ta mamaye rabin kasar, dake kudanci a karni na goma sha shida sarkin masarautar taglag ya haka al'adun addinin hindu da addinin [Musulunci](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musulunci "Musulunci") da haka ya iya ya hukunta kasar ta indiya.

bHarat

Tun da turawa suka gane hanyar ruwa ta zuwa [Indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya) sukai ta shigowa musamman ma mutanen [Faransa](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faransa "Faransa") da na [Portugal](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal "Portugal") da na [Birtaniya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birtaniya "Birtaniya") sun sha kama karfe da karfi kuwa yana son ya mamaye wannan kasa, wanda keda yawan arziki sun na son mamaye [indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya) dan saboda yawan yaki a junan su, amma turawa sun haɗa kawunan su don su sace arzikin kasar a shekara ta dubu daya da dari takwas da hamsin da bakwai 1857 'yan [indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya) suka yiwa turawa tawaye duk da haka turawa sun samu nasara a kan 'yan tawaye magaye tun daga wannan lokaci [Indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya) tazama a hannun turawa musamman ma tajmahal, duk dahaka [Mahatma Gandhi](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahatma_Gandhi "Mahatma Gandhi") ya yi kungiya tana neman yancin kasa dahaka turawa suka yi alkawarin zasu basu 'yanci a ranar 8 ga watan Agusta (1949) kuma a ranar ashirin da shida (26) ga watan Janairu shekarar dubu daya da dari tara da hamsin (1950) [Indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya) tazama Jamhuriya. [B. R. Ambedkar](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/B._R._Ambedkar "B. R. Ambedkar") ya rubuta kundin tsarin mulkin Indiya mai cin gashin kansa, kuma ya kafa tushen dimokuradiyya a [Indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya).

Saboda yawancin addinai da yawan Kabiloli ta samu kanta a cikin yakin basasa a shekara ta dubu daya da dari tara da saba'in da biyar (1975) zuwa shekarar dubu daya da dari tara da saba'in da bakwai (1977) lokacin hukunci Indira Gandhi ta hana fitar dare a wannan lokacin Indiya tazama kasa ce mai bin tsarin dimukuradiya da na jamhuriya.

[Indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya) ta sha yaki da makutan ta akan iyaka kawa kasar cin a shekara ta (1962) kuma da [pakistan](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan "Pakistan") har sau hudu a shekara ta (1947), (1964)(1971).[[1]](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya#cite_note-1)[[2]](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya#cite_note-2)

## Jinsin Halitta:

A cikin kasar [indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya) akwai maza da mata, farare da bakake, munana da masu kyau, sai dai mata sunfi maza yawa a cikin [Indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya), kuma yawancin matan [Indiya](https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiya) kyawawa ne, suna da gashi, kuma suna da san kwalliya.

## Al'adu:
