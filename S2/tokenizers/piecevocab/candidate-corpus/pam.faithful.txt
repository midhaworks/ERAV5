Ing **Republica ning India** ([Hindi](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi "Hindi") भारत गणराज्य *[Bhārata Gaṇarājya](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhāratavarsha?action=edit&redlink=1 "Bhāratavarsha")*; lawen ya rin [aliwang lagyu](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_country_names_in_various_languages_(D-I)?action=edit&redlink=1#I "List of country names in various languages (D-I)")), kebaluan bilang **India**, metung yang sovereign a bangsa king [Mauling Asia](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauling_Asia "Mauling Asia"). Iti ing pang[pitung-pekamaragul](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_outlying_territories_by_total_area?action=edit&redlink=1 "List of countries and outlying territories by total area") a bangsa karas keng dagul ningkayang geografia, ing [pangaduang pekamarakal a populasyun](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_population?action=edit&redlink=1 "List of countries by population") a bangsa, at pekamarakal a populasyun a [liberal democracy](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_democracy?action=edit&redlink=1 "Liberal democracy") keti yatu. Ding sepu na ilapin deng [Indian Ocean](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Ocean "Indian Ocean") king mauli, ing [Arabian Dayat Malat](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabian_Dayat_Malat?action=edit&redlink=1 "Arabian Dayat Malat") king albugan, ing [Bay ning Bengal](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_ning_Bengal?action=edit&redlink=1 "Bay ning Bengal") king aslagan, ing India atin yang pampang a maiggit 7000 kilometru. Ding sepu na ilapin ding [Pakistan](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan "Pakistan") king albuganb;[[1]](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/India#cite_note-Afghanistan-1) [Tsina](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Republic_of_China "People's Republic of China"), [Nepal](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal "Nepal"), at [Bhutan](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhutan "Bhutan") king pangulung-aslagan; at [Bangladesh](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh") at [Myanmar](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar "Myanmar") king aslagan. King Indian Ocean, ing India atiu mu karin ing [Sri Lanka](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka "Sri Lanka"), [Maldives](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maldives "Maldives") at [Indonesia](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia "Indonesia").

## Pangadake ning India

:   *Pun bulung: [Subdivisions of India](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Subdivisions of India")*

Ing India metung yang pisanmetung a aduang-pulung [states](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_(bangsang_pangadake)?action=edit&redlink=1 "State (bangsang pangadake)") at pitung federal a gubyernu. Deng ngan states, deng union territoryu ning Puducherry, at ing Bangsang Tungku Territoryu ning Delhi atin lang meyalal a gubyernu. Detang aliwang limang territoryu atin lang mitulduan a administrador.

1. [↑](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/India#cite_ref-Afghanistan_1-0) The Government of India also considers Afghanistan to be a bordering country. This is because it considers the entire state of [Jammu and Kashmir](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_and_Kashmir?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jammu and Kashmir") to be a part of India including the portion bordering [Afghanistan](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghanistan "Afghanistan"). A ceasefire sponsored by the [United Nations](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations "United Nations") in 1948 froze the positions of Indian and [Pakistani](https://pam.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan "Pakistan") held territory. As a consequence, the region bordering Afghanistan is in Pakistani-administered territory.
