India (Bharat, Hindustan), South [Asia](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia "Asia") ka ek desh hae. Iski abaadi 1,370,000,000 se jaada hae. India ki raajdhaani [New Delhi](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Delhi "New Delhi") hae. Is desh ki 22 adhikarik bhashayen hae. Ye kshetrafal me duniya ka satvaan (7th) sab se bada desh hae aur abaadi mein sab se bada desh hae, aur duniya ki sab se barraa democracy hae. Iske dakshin me [Indian Ocean](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Ocean "Indian Ocean"), pashchim me [Arabian Sea](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabian_Sea "Arabian Sea"), purab me [Bay of Bengal](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_of_Bengal "Bay of Bengal") hae. Iske border ke paschim me [Pakistan](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan "Pakistan"), uttar me [China](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/China "China"), [Bhutan](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhutan "Bhutan"), [Nepal](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal "Nepal") aur poorab me [Bangladesh](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh") aur [Myanmar](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar "Myanmar") hae. India ke dakshin me [Indian Ocean](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Ocean "Indian Ocean") me to the east. India ke dakshin me [Sri Lanka](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka "Sri Lanka"), [Maldives](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maldives "Maldives"), aur [Indonesia](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia "Indonesia") hae.

India ke ruling ke ithiaas ke bare mein kaha gaya hae ki Chandragupt Murya India ke pehle Raja the aur modern day India ko banane mein Shri Jawaharlal Nehru ka haath hae magar Shri Dr. Rajendra Prasad bhi ho sakte hae British ke madam se. Aur dusre aise ithasik log hae Ashoka the Great. Turko Indian Tuqhlaq aur Mughal Empire ka bhi haath hae, Indus valley civilization. Aaj ke zamane mein [All India National Congress](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_India_National_Congress "All India National Congress") aur [Bharatiya Janata Party](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bharatiya_Janata_Party "Bharatiya Janata Party") aur Janata Dal aur communist party ka Hukum ke barabari chalta hae aur raja aur politician ke bhi haath hae.

[India](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Settler "Indian Settler") me 28 states aur 9 union territories hae (jisme national capital territory bhi hae)[[1]](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/India#cite_note-1). Union territories ke President of India se appoint karaa gais administrators chalawe hae. Dui territor, (Delhi aur Puducherry) ke partial statehood dewa gais hae, jisme ek elected legislature aur executive councils of ministers hae, lekin state e raam ii logan ke lage puura taagat nai hae.Article 370 5 August ko remove kiya tha aur tab 9 union terretories ho gaye.

## Etymology

India ke naam Indus se aais hae, jon ki purana Farsi bhasa me Hindu sabd hae. Farsi me ii Sanskrit सिन्धु (Sindhu) se aais hae, jisme ii Indus Naddi ke naam hae. Purana jamana ke Greeek logan Hindusani log ke Indoi (Ινδοί), yaaniki Indus ke log, bolate the. India ke Constitution me des ko Bharat (ucharan: [ˈbʱaːrət̪]) bola jata hae. Bharat naam India ke ek purana raja Bharata se aya hae. Hindustan ([ɦɪnd̪ʊˈst̪aːn]) , jo ki Farsi se aaya hae, ka matalab “Hinduyon ka desh” hae.

## Itihaas

India ek prachin des hae jiska lamba itihas hae. Adhunik India 1947 se ajaad desh hae,jo pehle British imperial crown aur usse pehle [East India company](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_India_company?action=edit&redlink=1 "East India company") ke indirect control Mein Tha.
India ke itihaas uu time se suruu hoe hae jab ki 500,000 saal pahle hian pe *[Homo erectus](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homo_erectus?action=edit&redlink=1 "Homo erectus")* rahat rahin, Iske baad hian pe 75,000 saal pahile *[Homo sapiens](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homo_sapiens?action=edit&redlink=1 "Homo sapiens")*rahe ke suruu karin. [Indus Valley Civilization](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus_Valley_Civilization "Indus Valley Civilization"), jon ki Indian sub-continent ke northwestern hissa me c. 3300 se 1300 BCE talak rahaa, India ke pahila khaas civilization rahaa. [Iron Age](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Age_India?action=edit&redlink=1 "Iron Age India") me ek [Vedic Civilization](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vedic_Period?action=edit&redlink=1 "Vedic Period"), [Indo-Gangetic plain](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Gangetic_plain?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indo-Gangetic plain") me suru bhais jisme [Magadha](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magadha "Magadha") kingdom rahaa jon ki [Mahavira](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahavira?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mahavira") aur [Gautama Buddha](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gautama_Buddha "Gautama Buddha") ke 6th or 5th century BCE me janam dis rahaa.

4th and 3rd centuries BCE me lagbhag subcontinent ke sab hissa ke [Maurya Empire](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurya_Empire "Maurya Empire") aapan niche kar liis. Iske baad 1500 chhota chhota kingdom, jiske middle ke nnam se jaana jaawe hae, rahaa. Ii time India ke economy jorr se barha rahaa. 4h century me India ke north aur cental hissa ke unite kar ke Gupta Empire banaa jon ki 200 saal talak chalaa. Ii time Hinduism aur aage barrha. South India me [Chalukyas](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalukya_dynasty?action=edit&redlink=1 "Chalukya dynasty"), [Cholas](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chola_dynasty "Chola dynasty"), [Pallavas](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pallavas?action=edit&redlink=1 "Pallavas"), aur [Pandyas](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandyan_Dynasty?action=edit&redlink=1 "Pandyan Dynasty") raj karat rahin jon ki [Hinduism](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism "Hinduism") aur [Buddhism](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism "Buddhism") ke [Asia](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia "Asia") ke dusra des me export karin.

South India me, [Kerala](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerala "Kerala") 77 CE se Roman Empire se trade karat rahaa. Islam bhi pahile Kerala me Muslim traders se aais. Muslim rule subcontinent me 712 CE me aais jab ki Arab general [Muhammad bin Qasim](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_bin_Qasim?action=edit&redlink=1 "Muhammad bin Qasim") [Sindh](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindh "Sindh") aur [Multan](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan?action=edit&redlink=1 "Multan") pr kapjaa karis.[[2]](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/India#cite_note-infopak-2) Iske baad dher aur Muslim invaders central Asia se aain aur [Delhi Sultanate](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Sultanate "Delhi Sultanate") aur [Mughal Empire](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mughal_Empire "Mughal Empire") jaise Empire ke suruu karin.

Mughal Empire ke saathe dher [Rajput](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajput "Rajput") kingdoms, aur dher Hindu states, jaise ki [Vijayanagara Empire](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijayanagara_Empire "Vijayanagara Empire"), [Maratha Empire](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maratha_Empire "Maratha Empire"), aur [Ahom Kingdom](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahom_Kingdom?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ahom Kingdom") bhi rahaa. 18th century me Moghul Empire [Afghans](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durrani_Empire?action=edit&redlink=1 "Durrani Empire"), [Balochis](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baloch_people?action=edit&redlink=1 "Baloch people"), [Sikhs](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikh_Empire?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sikh Empire"), aur Marathas ke kaaran kamjor hoe gais. .[[3]](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/India#cite_note-east_india-3)

Mid-18th century ke baad, India ke dhire dhire British East India Company aapan niche kar liis. Company ke raaj se nakhusi ke kaaran [Indian Rebellion of 1857](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Rebellion_of_1857?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian Rebellion of 1857") bhais aur iske baad , India ke was directly [British Crown](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Raj?action=edit&redlink=1 "British Raj") ke niche karaa gais. Iske baad [struggle for independence](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_independence_movement?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian independence movement") ke [Indian National Congress](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Congress "Indian National Congress") suru karis jiske baad me [Muslim League](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/All-India_Muslim_League?action=edit&redlink=1 "All-India Muslim League") bhi join karis. Subcontinent ke [United Kingdom](https://hif.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom "United Kingdom") se 1947 me ajaadi mila aur des ke partition kar ke India aur Pakistan banawa gais.

### Notes

- \* Interim - It is debatable whether Mr. Gulzari Lal Nanda was a Prime Minister or an Acting Prime Minister. The constitution does not have a position of acting Prime Minister. Mr. Nanda was sworn in as the Prime Minister of India. In contrast, the constitution provides for an acting President who discharges the duties of a President. Hence, constitutional experts and historians now agree that Mr. Nanda is the second Prime Minister of India.
