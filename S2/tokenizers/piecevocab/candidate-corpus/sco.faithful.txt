**Indie** ([Hindi](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi_leid "Hindi leid"): भारत
Category: ./Category:Airticles\_conteenin\_Hindi-leid\_text
, *bhārat*
Category: ./Category:Airticles\_wi\_text\_from\_the\_Indic\_leids\_collective
), offeecially the **Republic o Indie** (Hindi: भारत गणराज्य
Category: ./Category:Airticles\_conteenin\_Hindi-leid\_text
, *bhārat gaṇrājya*
Category: ./Category:Airticles\_wi\_text\_from\_the\_Indic\_leids\_collective
; [Inglish](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inglis_leid "Inglis leid"): *Republic of India*
Category: ./Category:Airticles\_conteenin\_Inglis-leid\_text
), is a kintra in [Sooth](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sooth_Asie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sooth Asie") [Asie](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asie "Asie"). It is the [seivent-lairgest](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leet_o_kintras_an_ootlyin_territories_bi_total_aurie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Leet o kintras an ootlyin territories bi total aurie") kintra bi geographical aurie, the [maist populous](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leet_o_kintras_bi_population "Leet o kintras bi population") kintra wi [ower 1.2 billion fowk](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_o_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Demographics o Indie"), an the maist populous democracy in the warld. [Mainland Indie](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainland_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mainland Indie") is boondit bi the [Indie Ocean](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_Ocean "Indie Ocean") on the sooth, the [Arabie Sea](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabie_Sea?action=edit&redlink=1 "Arabie Sea") on the wast, an the [Bay o Bengal](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_o_Bengal "Bay o Bengal") on the east; an it is bordered bi [Pakistan](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan "Pakistan") tae the wast;[[note]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#endnote_Anone) [Bhutan](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhutan "Bhutan"), the [Fowkrepublic o Cheenae](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fowkrepublic_o_Cheenae "Fowkrepublic o Cheenae") an [Nepal](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal "Nepal") tae the north; an [Bangladesh](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh") an [Burma](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burma "Burma") tae the east. In the Indie Ocean, mainland Indie an the [Lakshadweep Islands](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakshadweep?action=edit&redlink=1 "Lakshadweep") are in the vicinity o [Sri Lanka](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka "Sri Lanka") an the [Maldives](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maldives "Maldives"), while Indie's [Andaman an Nicobar Islands](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andaman_an_Nicobar_Islands "Andaman an Nicobar Islands") share maritime mairch wi [Thailand](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thailand "Thailand") an the [Indonesian](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesie "Indonesie") island o [Sumatra](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aceh "Aceh") in the [Andaman Sea](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andaman_Sea?action=edit&redlink=1 "Andaman Sea").[[13]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#cite_note-15) Indie haes a coastline o 7,517 kilometres (4,700 mi).[[14]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#cite_note-sanilkumar-16)

Hame tae the auncient [Indus Valley Ceevilisation](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus_Valley_Ceevilisation?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indus Valley Ceevilisation") an a region o historic tred routes an vast empires, the [Indie subcontinent](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_subcontinent?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indie subcontinent") wis identified wi its commercial an cultural wealth for muckle o its lang history.[[15]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#cite_note-17) Fower o the warld's major releegions—[Hinduism](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism_in_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Hinduism in Indie"), [Buddhism](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism_in_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Buddhism in Indie"), [Jainism](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jainism_in_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jainism in Indie") an [Sikhism](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikhism_in_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sikhism in Indie")—oreeginatit here, while [Zoroastrianism](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parsi?action=edit&redlink=1 "Parsi"), [Judaism](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judaism_in_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Judaism in Indie"), [Christianity](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity_in_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Christianity in Indie") an [Islam](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Islam in Indie") arrived in the first millennium [CE](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Era?action=edit&redlink=1 "Common Era") an shapit the region's [diverse cultur](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_cultur?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian cultur"). Gradually [annexed](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Company_rule_in_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Company rule in Indie") bi the [Breetish East Indie Company](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Indie_Company?action=edit&redlink=1 "East Indie Company") frae the early 18t century an [colonised](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breetish_Raj "Breetish Raj") bi the Unitit Kinrick frae the mid-19t century, Indie became an independent naition in 1947 efter a [struggle for unthirldom](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_unthirldom_movement?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indian unthirldom movement") which wis merked bi a [non-violent resistance](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-violent_resistance?action=edit&redlink=1 "Non-violent resistance") led bi [Mahatma Gandhi](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohandas_Karamchand_Gandhi "Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi").

Indie is a [federal](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federalism?action=edit&redlink=1 "Federalism") [constitutional republic](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_republic "Constitutional republic") wi a [pairlamentary democracy](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pairlamentary_seestem "Pairlamentary seestem") consistin o [28 states an seiven union territories](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_an_territories_o_Indie "States an territories o Indie"). A [pluralistic](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Releegious_pluralism?action=edit&redlink=1 "Releegious pluralism"), [multilingual](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leids_o_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Leids o Indie") an multiethnic society whaur mair nor 300[[16]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#cite_note-18) leids are spoken, Indie is an aa hame tae a diversity o [wildlife](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fauna_o_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Fauna o Indie") in a variety o [pertected habitats](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protectit_aurie_o_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Protectit aurie o Indie"). The [Indian economy](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy_o_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Economy o Indie") is the warld's [eleivent lairgest economy](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leet_o_kintras_bi_GDP_(nominal)?action=edit&redlink=1 "Leet o kintras bi GDP (nominal)") bi nominal [GDP](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product "Gross domestic product") an the [fowert lairgest](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leet_o_kintras_bi_GDP_(PPP)?action=edit&redlink=1 "Leet o kintras bi GDP (PPP)") bi [purchasin pouer parity](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purchasin_pouer_parity?action=edit&redlink=1 "Purchasin pouer parity"). Syne the introduction of [mercatt-based economic reforms](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_liberalisation_in_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Economic liberalisation in Indie") in 1991, Indie haes acome ane o the [fastest growin major economies](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leet_o_kintra_bi_GDP_(real)_growth_rate?action=edit&redlink=1 "Leet o kintra bi GDP (real) growth rate") in the warld;[[17]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#cite_note-ERS-19) housomeivver, the kintra continues tae face several [poverty](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_in_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Poverty in Indie"), [illiteracy](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literacy_in_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Literacy in Indie"), [corruption](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corruption_in_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Corruption in Indie") an [public heal](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_in_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Health in Indie") relatit challenges. Indie is clessifee'd as a [newly industrialised kintra](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newly_industrialised_kintra?action=edit&redlink=1 "Newly industrialised kintra") an is ane o the fower [BRIC](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/BRIC?action=edit&redlink=1 "BRIC") naitions.[[18]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#cite_note-UNIDO_Industry-20)[[19]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#cite_note-Limits-21) It is the warld's saxt *de facto* [recognised](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S.-India_Civil_Nuclear_Cooperation_Initiative?action=edit&redlink=1#NSG_waiver "U.S.-India Civil Nuclear Cooperation Initiative") [nuclear weapons state](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_weapons_state?action=edit&redlink=1 "Nuclear weapons state") an haes the [third-lairgest staundin airmed force](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leet_o_kintras_bi_number_o_troops?action=edit&redlink=1 "Leet o kintras bi number o troops") in the warld, while its [military expenditure ranks tent](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leet_o_kintras_bi_military_expenditures?action=edit&redlink=1 "Leet o kintras bi military expenditures") in the warld.[[20]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#cite_note-India-22) Indie is a [regional pouer](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_pouer?action=edit&redlink=1 "Regional pouer") in Sooth Asie.[[21]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#cite_note-23)

It is a [foondin member](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_state?action=edit&redlink=1 "Member state") o the [Unitit Naitions](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitit_Naitions "Unitit Naitions"), the [Non-Aligned Movement](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Aligned_Movement "Non-Aligned Movement"), the [Warld Tred Organisation](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warld_Tred_Organisation?action=edit&redlink=1 "Warld Tred Organisation"), the [Sooth Asian Association for Regional Cooperation](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sooth_Asian_Association_for_Regional_Cooperation?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sooth Asian Association for Regional Cooperation"), the [East Asie Summit](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Asie_Summit?action=edit&redlink=1 "East Asie Summit"), the [G20](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/G20 "G20") an the [G8+5](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/G8+5?action=edit&redlink=1 "G8+5"); a member o the [Commonweel o Naitions](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonweel_o_Naitions "Commonweel o Naitions"); an an observer state in the [Shanghai Cooperation Organisation](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Cooperation_Organisation?action=edit&redlink=1 "Shanghai Cooperation Organisation").

## Etymologie

Main airticle: [Names o Indie](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Names_o_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Names o Indie")

The name *Indie* is derivit frae *[Indus](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus_River?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indus River")*, which is derivit frae the [Auld Persian](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auld_Persian?action=edit&redlink=1 "Auld Persian") wird *[Hindu](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu "Hindu")*, frae [Sanskrit](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit "Sanskrit") सिन्धु *Sindhu*, the historic local appellation for the [Indus River](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus_River?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indus River").[[22]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#cite_note-24) The auncient [Greeks](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greeks "Greeks") referred tae the Indians as *Indoi* (Ινδοί), the fowk o the Indus.[[23]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#cite_note-basham-25) The [Constitution o Indie](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_o_Indie?action=edit&redlink=1 "Constitution o Indie") an common uisage in various Indian leids an aa recognise *Bharat* (pronoonced [[ˈbʱaːrət̪]](Wikipedia:IPA_for_Hindi_and_Urdu?action=edit&redlink=1 "Wikipedia:IPA for Hindi and Urdu") ([![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/21/Speaker_Icon.svg/20px-Speaker_Icon.svg.png)](File:Hi-Bharat.ogg "File:Hi-Bharat.ogg") [listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/79/Hi-Bharat.ogg "Hi-Bharat.ogg"))) as an offeecial name o equal status.[[24]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#cite_note-26) The name Bharat is derivit frae the name o the legendary king [Bharata](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bharata_(emperor)?action=edit&redlink=1 "Bharata (emperor)") in Hindu scriptures. *[Hindustan](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindustan "Hindustan")* ([[ɦɪnd̪ʊˈst̪aːn]](Wikipedia:IPA_for_Hindi_and_Urdu?action=edit&redlink=1 "Wikipedia:IPA for Hindi and Urdu") ([![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/21/Speaker_Icon.svg/20px-Speaker_Icon.svg.png)](File:Hindustan.ogg "File:Hindustan.ogg") [listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/46/Hindustan.ogg "Hindustan.ogg"))), oreeginally a [Persian](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_leid "Persian leid") wird for “Land o the Hindus” referrin tae [northren Indie](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Indie "North Indie"), is an aa occasionally uised as a synonym for aw o Indie.[[25]](https://sco.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie#cite_note-27)
