**Idia** (Hindi: Bhārat), ka a tu kina na **Ripabuliki ni Idia** (Hindi: Bhārat Gaṇarājya), e dua na matanitu mai na [Ceva Esia](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceva_Esia?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ceva Esia"). E ikavitu ni matanitu levu duadua ka levu duadua na tamata e vuravura ena demokarasi. Vauci mai na [Wasa Idia](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasa_Idia?action=edit&redlink=1 "Wasa Idia") ena ceva, na [Wasawasa Arapea](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasawasa_Arapea?action=edit&redlink=1 "Wasawasa Arapea") ena cevara, kei na [toba ni Bengal](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toba_ni_Bengal?action=edit&redlink=1 "Toba ni Bengal") ena cevatokalau, ka wasea na iyalayala ni vanua ki na [Pakisitani](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakisitani?action=edit&redlink=1 "Pakisitani") ki na ra; [Jaina](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaina_(matanitu) "Jaina (matanitu)"), [Nepal](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal "Nepal"), kei [Bhutan](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhutan?action=edit&redlink=1 "Bhutan") ki na vualiku; kei [Bangladesh](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh") kei [Myanmar](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar?action=edit&redlink=1 "Myanmar") ki na tokalau. Ena [Wasa Idia](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasa_Idia?action=edit&redlink=1 "Wasa Idia"), o Idia e tiko ena a ni [Sri Lanka](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sri Lanka") kei [Maldives](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maldives?action=edit&redlink=1 "Maldives"); rau wasea o [Andaman](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andaman?action=edit&redlink=1 "Andaman") kei [Nicobar Yanuyanu](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicobar_Yanuyanu?action=edit&redlink=1 "Nicobar Yanuyanu") e dua na iyalayala maritime kei [Thailand](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thailand "Thailand") kei [Indonesia](https://fj.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia "Indonesia").
