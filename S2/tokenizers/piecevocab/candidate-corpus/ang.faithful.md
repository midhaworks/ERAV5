**Indea land** ([Hindisc](https://ang.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindisc_sprǣc "Hindisc sprǣc"): भारत, ***Bhārat***; sēo ēac [ōðere naman](https://ang.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambihtlice_naman_Indian?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ambihtlice naman Indian")) is land in sūðernre [Asie](https://ang.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asie "Asie"). Indea rīce, ambihtlīċe sēo **Indie Cyneƿīse**, is selfƿealdend land.

Indea Cyneƿise is sē mǣsta dǣl þæs [Indiscan Healfƿorulddǣles](https://ang.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indisc_Healfworulddǣl?action=edit&redlink=1 "Indisc Healfworulddǣl").
