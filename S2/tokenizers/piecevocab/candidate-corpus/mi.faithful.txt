Ko **Īnia** ([reo Hīni](https://mi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reo_Hīni "Reo Hīni"): भारत गणराज्य, *Bhārat Gaṇarājya*) he whenua nui ki te tonga o [Āhia](https://mi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Āhia "Āhia"); he nui rawa ngā reo o reira, nō te mea, he nui ake i te 1,210,193,422 te taupori o taua whenua.
