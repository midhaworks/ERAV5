**Yindu**[[1]](https://za.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yindu#cite_note-1) roxnaeuz **Yaenq Doh** (印度, [Vah Yindi](https://za.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vah_Yindi "Vah Yindi"):भारत गणराज्य) dwg aen guekgya youq baihnamz [yacouh](https://za.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yacouh "Yacouh") ndeu. De miz gijgvangq 316 fanh, daengx seiqgyaiq ndawde baiz daih caet. Daengz bi 2022, haeujvunz miz 14 ik, baiz daih ngeih. Baihnamz de caeuq [Yinduyangz](https://za.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yinduyangz "Yinduyangz") gaenhlaenz, baihbaek laenz [Ahlahboz Haij](https://za.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahlahboz_Haij?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ahlahboz Haij"), haijansen miz 7517 goengleix. Baihsae de dwg [Bahgihswhdanj](https://za.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahgihswhdanj?action=edit&redlink=1 "Bahgihswhdanj"), baihbaek miz [Cunghvaz Yinzminz Gunghozgoz](https://za.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cunghvaz_Yinzminz_Gunghozgoz "Cunghvaz Yinzminz Gunghozgoz"), [Nizbozwj](https://za.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizbozwj?action=edit&redlink=1 "Nizbozwj") caeuq [Budanh](https://za.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budanh "Budanh"). Soujduh de dwg [Moq Dwzlij](https://za.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moq_Dwzlij "Moq Dwzlij").
