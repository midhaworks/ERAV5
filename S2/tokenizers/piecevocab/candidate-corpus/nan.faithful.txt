**Ìn-tō͘ Kiōng-hô-kok** (印度共和國, [Eng-gí](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eng-gí "Eng-gí"): Republic of India, [Hindi-gí](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi-gí "Hindi-gí"): भारत गणराज्य; [ISO](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_15919 "ISO 15919"): Bhārat Gaṇarājya[[3]](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ìn-tō͘#cite_note-3)), kán-chheng **Ìn-tō͘**, tī [A-chiu](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-chiu "A-chiu") tāi-lio̍k ê lâm-pêng. Sī choân sè-kài siāng tōa ê [chū-iû-bîn-chú](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chū-iû_bîn-chú "Chū-iû bîn-chú") kok-ka, [jîn-kháu](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jîn-kháu "Jîn-kháu") chhiau-kòe 14-ek (sè-kài siāng chē--ê), [gí-giân](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gí-giân "Gí-giân") iok-lio̍k 900 chióng. Chiū bêng-gī [GDP](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP "GDP") lâi khòaⁿ, Ìn-tō͘ sī sè-kài [tē-5 tōa ê keng-chè-thé](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kok-ka_bêng-gī_GDP_lia̍t-toaⁿ "Kok-ka bêng-gī GDP lia̍t-toaⁿ").

Ìn-tō͘ piⁿ--a ū [Pakistan](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan "Pakistan"), [Tiong-hôa Jîn-bîn Kiōng-hô-kok](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiong-hôa_Jîn-bîn_Kiōng-hô-kok "Tiong-hôa Jîn-bîn Kiōng-hô-kok"), [Bián-tiān](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bián-tiān "Bián-tiān"), [Bangladesh](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh"), [Nepal](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal "Nepal"), [Bhutan](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhutan "Bhutan"), [Afghanistan](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghanistan "Afghanistan"). Ìn-tō͘ ê lâm-hong sī [Ìn-tō͘-iûⁿ](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ìn-tō͘-iûⁿ "Ìn-tō͘-iûⁿ"), hái-siōng ê lín-kok ū [Sri Lanka](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka "Sri Lanka"), [Maldives](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maldives "Maldives") kap [Ìn-nî](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ìn-nî "Ìn-nî") chia ê tó-kok.

[Liân-ha̍p Ông-kok](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liân-ha̍p_Ông-kok "Liân-ha̍p Ông-kok") chèng-sek thóng-tī Ìn-tō͘ beh ū 90 tang, [1947 nî](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/1947_nî "1947 nî") [to̍k-li̍p](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ìn-tō͘_to̍k-li̍p_ūn-tōng "Ìn-tō͘ to̍k-li̍p ūn-tōng").

## Tē-lí

Ìn-tō͘ ê chóng bīn-chek 318 bān pêng-hong kong-lí, sī sè-kài tē 7 tōa, chiàm [Ìn-tō͘ chù-tāi-lio̍k](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ìn-tō͘_chhù-tāi-lio̍k "Ìn-tō͘ chhù-tāi-lio̍k") ê tōa pō͘-hūn.

## Hêng-chèng-khu

Siông-sè chhiáⁿ khoàⁿ: [Ìn-tō͘ ê hêng-chèng-khu](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ìn-tō͘_ê_hêng-chèng-khu "Ìn-tō͘ ê hêng-chèng-khu")

Ìn-tō͘ ê hoat-lu̍t siōng oe̍h-hun-chò 28 ê pang (प्रांत, pradeshs) kah 8 ê liân-pang sio̍k-tē (केन्द्र शासित प्रदेश, union territories, pau-koat kok-ka siú-to͘ Sin Delhi). Kî-tiong [Arunachal Pradesh](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachal_Pradesh "Arunachal Pradesh"), [Ladakh](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladakh "Ladakh") kah [Jammu kap Kashmir](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_kap_Kashmir "Jammu kap Kashmir") tiong-iong ti̍t-hat-khu sī ū cheng-gī--ê.

## Chèng-tī

Ìn-tō͘ sī chi̍t-ê [liân-pang kiōng-hô-kok](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liân-pang_Kiōng-hô-kok?action=edit&redlink=1 "Liân-pang Kiōng-hô-kok"). Ū 7.14 ek ê soán-bîn, 1,000 gōa ê chèng-tóng chham-ú kok-hōe soán-kí: pau-koat 7 ê choân-kok-sèng tōa-tóng (chèng-tóng liân-bêng), 40 ê tē-khu chèng-tóng, iah-koh-ū 980 ê sió-tóng. Soan-chheng sī choân-sè-kài siōng tōa kui-bô͘ ê bîn-chú soán-kí (2009 nî).

[Ìn-tō͘ chóng-thóng](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ìn-tō͘_Chóng-thóng?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ìn-tō͘ Chóng-thóng") sī [kok-ka goân-siú](https://zh-min-nan.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kok-ka_Goân-siú?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kok-ka Goân-siú"), mā sī bú-chong pō͘-tūi ê thóng-sòe. Chóng-thóng sī hi-khoân--ê, pêng-siông-sî iû chóng-lí chióng-ak si̍t-khoân. Iah-m̄-koh, nā sī chèng-hú siū-tio̍h chiàn-cheng ê ui-hia̍p, chhim-lio̍k, ia̍h-sī po̍k-tōng ê sî, chóng-thóng ū-khoân soan-pò͘ kín-kip chōng-thài; nā sī chèng-hú poàn-sūi--khì, chóng-thóng ē-tàng tāi-hêng chèng-hú siú-náu ê chit-lêng. Tong gī-hōe soán-kí hām-ji̍p kiong-kio̍k ê sî-chūn, chóng-thóng ē-sái chí-tēng chèng-tóng lâi cho͘-koh, lî-chhiáⁿ i ē-sái hóⁿ-koat chèng-hú thê-chhut ê hoat-àn. Chóng-thóng chham hù-chóng-thóng ê jīm-kî sī 5 tang: In sī iû chi̍t-ê te̍k-siat ê soán-kí ki-kò͘ kàn-chiap soán-kí lâi sán-seng. Ìn-tō͘ hù-chóng-thóng tī-leh chóng-thóng bô-hoat-tō͘ hêng-sú koân-le̍k ê sî-chūn, sī bē-tàng chū-tōng chiap-jīm chóng-thóng ê ūi--ê.
