**India** (bhâsa Inggris: *India; Republic of India*, bhâsa Hindi: *Bhārat; Bhārat Gaṇarājya*) panèka sala sèttong naghârâ è [Asia Lao'](https://mad.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Lao'?action=edit&redlink=1 "Asia Lao'"). Embu' koṭṭana iyâ arèya [New Delhi](https://mad.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Delhi?action=edit&redlink=1 "New Delhi") bân koṭṭa palèng rajâ iyâ arèya [Mumbai](https://mad.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mumbai"). Loas bilajâhna iyâ arèya 3,287,263 km². Pendudukna iyâ arèya 1,393,409,038 (2021) bân kapadatan pendudukna 410 orèng per km².[[1]](https://mad.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inḍia#cite_note-1)
